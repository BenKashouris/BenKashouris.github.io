About

Ben Kashouris

This summer, I completed an LMS-funded research project at the University of Nottingham, exploring applications of random matrix methods to high-dimensional machine learning. I achieved averages of 90% in my first year and 85% in my second year.

Alongside my degree, I co-founded Amphora, which won the Steve Jobs Award from Queens' College, Cambridge and we hope to launch soon.

I originally studied Computer Science before moving to mathematics, and programming remains an important part of how I work. I mainly use Python and Haskell to build simulations, visualisations and computational experiments.
